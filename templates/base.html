Welcome to StudyMate!

Your personal AI-powered study assistant for high school, university, and self-learners.
Here is how to get the most out of it.

AI Study Assistant Ask any academic question regardless of your level. Definitions, explanations, past questions, or concepts you do not understand. The AI adapts to your level and remembers your conversation history so you can always continue where you left off.
SP
Study Plan Generator Enter your subjects and select the exact days and times you are free to study. The AI will build a detailed personalised timetable that fits your real schedule, not a random one.
PT
Performance Tracker Visit the Performance page to see how many questions you have asked, study plans generated, and quizzes taken. Use this to stay consistent and monitor your progress over time.
QZ
AI Quiz Generator Test your knowledge on any topic. Enter a subject, choose your level and number of questions, and the AI will generate a multiple choice quiz and score you instantly.
TIP
Pro Tip For best results, be specific when asking the AI. Instead of "explain chemistry", try "explain the difference between ionic and covalent bonding with examples." The more detail you give, the better the answer.
